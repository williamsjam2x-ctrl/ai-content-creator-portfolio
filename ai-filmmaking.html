CASE STUDY · AI FILMMAKING LABORATORY

Turn a film idea into a coherent production plan.

A practical pre-production method for scene purpose, camera language, sound, visual continuity and review. AI supports the craft. The story and viewer experience guide the decisions.

Production problem

AI-assisted video sequences often feel disconnected when character positions, props, lighting, camera direction and sound change without a story reason. A prompt alone does not replace a production plan.

My planning process

01

Define the audience experience

Choose what the viewer should feel, understand or wonder about in each beat. Give every scene one clear purpose.

02

Break the story into shots

Set shot size, angle, camera movement, subject action, screen direction and the transition into the next beat.

03

Lock continuity details

Record wardrobe, props, positions, time of day, light direction and environmental details. Keep them stable unless the story changes them.

04

Design the sound

Plan diegetic sound from the scene, non-diegetic music, atmosphere, pauses and narration. Voice-over should add point of view or context, not repeat visible action.

05

Review the edit

Check whether each shot advances the story, whether transitions make sense, and whether sound, pacing and captions support the intended experience.

Example shot plan

BeatPicture & cameraSound & purpose
1. EstablishWide shot shows the location and key spatial relationships. Camera remains steady.Natural ambience establishes place and mood.
2. RevealSlow push-in reveals the important object or action without changing prop positions.A focused sound cue draws attention to the discovery.
3. Human detailMedium or close shot shows a meaningful reaction or practical detail.Dialogue or restrained voice-over adds context.
4. PayoffReturn to the key action or a wider view that resolves the question.Music resolves only if the story earns it. Leave space for the moment.

This is a planning example, not a claim that a finished film was produced from this shot list.
