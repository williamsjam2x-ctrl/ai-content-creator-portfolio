CASE STUDY · WORKFLOW DESIGN01 / 03

AI Content Production Pipeline

The problem: Content work gets messy when research, scripts, media, review and publishing are handled without clear handoffs. Missing details or rushed checks can create avoidable rework.

My approach: I mapped a staged workflow from brief and research to script, media, quality checks, human approval and post-publication review. Each stage has an expected output and a check before the next handoff.

Stage-based workflowInput validationError recovery planAudit trailHuman approval

What this demonstrates: I think beyond a prompt. I consider what enters a workflow, how errors are caught, who approves public output and what record is kept.
