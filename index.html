AI AUTOMATION · WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE · CONTENT OPERATIONS

SI Automation Pipeline

A modular content workflow designed to make production easier to track, test and improve—not a one-click content factory.

Brief & research→Script & prompts→Media production→QA & approval→Publish & learn

Architecture

Orchestration layer: coordinates tools and moves a job through defined stages. Data and asset layer: stores briefs, versions, media references and status. Validation layer: checks required fields, evidence, continuity, rights and delivery quality. Governance layer: records errors, retries, decisions and approval. Human control: blocks public release until an authorised person approves it.

Evidence stage: architecture and workflow design. Production integrations and end-to-end results will be added only after they are tested and recorded.
