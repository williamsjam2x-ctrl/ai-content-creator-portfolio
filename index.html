CASE STUDY · WORKFLOW DESIGN01 / 03

AI Automation Pipeline

The problem: Content work gets messy when research, scripts, media, review and publishing are handled without clear handoffs. Missing details or rushed checks can create avoidable rework.

My approach: I designed a staged workflow from brief and research through scripting, media production, validation, error recovery, quality checks, human approval and post-publication review. Each stage has defined outputs and checks before handoff.

Workflow architectureInput validationError recoveryAudit trailHuman approval

What this demonstrates: I approach automation as a managed process: defined inputs, validation gates, recovery paths, accountable approval and traceable records—not just a sequence of prompts.

SCRIPTWRITING SAMPLE01

AI Movie Behind-the-Scenes Prompt Framework

Purpose: Turn a film concept into a coherent behind-the-scenes sequence that feels like a real production environment.

Production elements: soundstage layout, practical effects and miniatures, camera setup, crew activity, timing, sound design, safety considerations and originality controls.

Scene logicCamera & blockingPractical effectsSound cuesNegative prompts

Sample direction: “Show the craft behind the shot: establish the soundstage, reveal the practical setup, then move closer to the crew coordinating the effect. Keep camera position, lighting direction and prop placement consistent between beats.”

Story-led video script structure

I build scripts around a strong opening question, a clear sequence of discoveries, visual evidence, purposeful narration and a satisfying payoff. Each scene has a job; narration adds context or mystery instead of merely describing what is already visible.
