CASE STUDY · STICKMAN EXPLAINERS

Simple visuals. Clear ideas. Strong story structure.

A 2D explainer approach for history, survival, biology and animal behaviour. The character style stays consistent while each scene helps the viewer understand a new point.

Back to creative workExplore filmmaking

Visual style

Original vector illustration created for this portfolio. It is a style sample, not a finished video frame.

  • Flat 2D artwork with a consistent character design.
  • Clear silhouettes and simple backgrounds.
  • Visuals explain the idea instead of decorating the narration.
  • No photorealistic or 3D style unless a project brief specifically calls for it.

Scene-building workflow

01

Choose the learning question

Define the question the viewer wants answered and the single takeaway the video must deliver.

02

Write the story beats

Use a hook, context, discoveries, explanation, consequence and payoff. Each beat changes what the viewer knows.

03

Assign a job to every visual

Show a process, compare two ideas, locate an event, reveal a risk or make an abstract point easier to understand.

04

Lock character and scene continuity

Keep character proportions, clothing, line weight, props and screen direction consistent across shots.

05

Audit the final sequence

Remove any shot that adds no understanding, emotion, discovery, meaning or story. Verify factual claims before delivery.

SAMPLE SCENE LOGIC

One visual, one clear purpose

Setup: Introduce the problem with a simple visual.

Discovery: Reveal the evidence or key difference.

Explanation: Show the mechanism step by step.

Payoff: Connect the explanation to the opening question.

The character style supports the lesson. It does not replace research, story structure or visual reasoning.

Typical deliverables

  • Topic and audience brief
  • Script with narration timing
  • Scene-by-scene visual plan
  • Character and style reference
  • Visual prompts and continuity notes
  • Fact-check and editing checklist
